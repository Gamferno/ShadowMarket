# ShadowMarket Proposal

## Executive Summary
Public prediction markets suffer from:
1. **Whale signaling**: Large public positions herd other bettors.
2. **Mempool front-running**: Bots exploit visible orders.
3. **Permanent doxxing**: Users cannot participate in sensitive civic or industry outcomes.

ShadowMarket addresses these challenges by shielding individual positions and only disclosing verified aggregate odds and fair payouts via zero-knowledge proofs on Midnight Network.
